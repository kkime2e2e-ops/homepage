About

Claims data · Cohort studies · Clinical data science

Undergraduate researcher using nationwide health insurance claims data (HIRA, Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service; NHIS, National Health Insurance Service) and statistical modeling in R and SQL to study how disease and treatment play out in routine care.

Research Interests

  • Clinical data science and predictive modeling
  • Pharmacoepidemiology using nationwide claims data
  • Data quality in real-world clinical databases

Research

  • Current Trends and 5-Year Recurrence Patterns of Inguinal Hernia Surgery in Korea: A Nationwide Study Using HIRA Data from 2006 to 2025

    A nationwide retrospective study of inguinal hernia repair using national claims data, tracking how surgical approach has shifted across two decades and which factors predict recurrence within five years. Cohort construction and analysis in R.

  • Rectal Prolapse and Rectocele Surgery in Korea: Nationwide Data from 2009 to 2023

    A nationwide analysis of surgical treatment for pelvic organ prolapse, examining the shift from perineal and open approaches toward minimally invasive surgery, along with differences in length of stay and healthcare costs. Trends analyzed by age, sex, region, and institution type in R.

  • Impact of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome on Long-Term Cardiovascular and Liver-Related Outcomes in Metabolic Dysfunction–Associated Steatotic Liver Disease: A Longitudinal Analysis Using National Health Insurance Data

    Examining whether polycystic ovary syndrome affects long-term outcomes in MASLD, using national health screening data. Combines descriptive cohort analysis with predictive modeling.
